# Aerodynamic Kit Design
## FSAE Aerodynamic Scoring
- Downforce, Drag and Center of Pressure (CP) targets <br/>
- Selection and sizing of aerodynamic components <br/>
- Downforce vs. drag vs. mass tradeoff <br/>
- Aerodynamic coefficients (CD, CL, etc.), reference area (A), and F = ½ρv²CA <br/>
- Elements: Front/rear/side wings, undertray, vortex generators, Gurney flaps, multi-element wings, etc. <br/>
- Component interaction: e.g. front wing effect on rear wing, connection of rear wing and undertray, driver’s head and roll hoop <br/>
- Angle of attack, chord length, camber, thickness; standard/tabulated airfoils, e.g., NACA <br/>
- Aero Maps: Downforce, drag, side force, moments; effects of ride heights, yaw angle, yaw rate, roll angle, steering <br/>
- Center of Pressure location, migration, and platform sensitivity; effect on vehicle balance, understeer/oversteer, relationship with CG <br/>
- Flow separation/stall, energized flow, slot gaps, boundary layers <br/>
- Ground effect, aeroelasticity, flutter, and active aero 

## Design Focus
- Determine the optimal center of pressure to support the team goal, increasing the operating range of the car and decreasing average lap times <br/>​
- Determine the amount of downforce and drag for improvement over no aerodynamic devices, to support the team goal of decreasing average lap times​​

## Responsibilities
- Experiment and determine the difference between regular wet layups and wet layups in a vacuum bag <br/>
- Run preliminary rear wing sims in Autodesk CFD to understand the relationship between the different variables that determine the performance of the rear wing <br/>
- Move simulations to ANSYS and find the best settings for our use case <br/>
- Assist in the wet layup manufacturing of the final rear wing <br/>
